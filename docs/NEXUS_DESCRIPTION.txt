[b]Elden Ring Duplicate Seller — offline beta[/b]

Press F8 to sell surplus carried equipment for the same rune value shown by traders, using the supplied price table or one extracted from your active regulation.

[b]What it keeps[/b]
• Two copies of each weapon/shield family.
• Two of each Ash of War.
• One of each armor piece and talisman ID.
• All consumables, spells, crafting materials, key items and ammunition.

Every equipped copy is protected, even above the normal keep count. Higher weapon upgrades are preferred next. Affinities count as the same weapon family. Different talisman tiers and altered armor IDs count separately. Items traders refuse to buy stay in your inventory. Storage is not processed.

[b]Requirements[/b]
Windows x64, Elden Ring executable 2.7.1.0 (in-game 1.17.1), Mod Engine 2 and the Microsoft Visual C++ 2015–2022 x64 runtime. Tested locally with Randomizer 0.12.2 and Seamless Co-op 2.0.1 loaded, with no other players connected. These optional mods are not included.

[b]Install[/b]
1. Close the game and back up your save.
2. Put the ERDuplicateSeller folder beside your Mod Engine 2 launcher.
3. Set sell_value_path in duplicate_seller.ini to the full path of the included sell_values.txt. Configure.ps1 can fill this in for you.
4. Add ERDuplicateSeller\\ERDuplicateSeller.dll to the existing external_dlls list in config_eldenring.toml. Keep your other DLL entries.
5. Launch offline through your modded launcher, load your character and press F8 while standing still.

The download starts in preview mode. Inspect the native_sale report beside the DLL, then set dry_run=false to enable selling. The included INSTALL.md has the complete setup, update and uninstall instructions.

[b]Prices and randomizers[/b]
The bundled numeric table comes from the installed base-game regulation. Its equipment/Ash prices match the locally tested randomizer profile. If your overhaul or randomizer changes prices or IDs, extract a new table from the regulation you actually load using the source repository helper. This mod does not replace regulation.bin.

[b]Beta status[/b]
The seller completed 111 verified catalog test batches, selling 2,538 surplus equipment/Ash copies for exactly 1,221,770 runes. Item caps prevented three-copy testing of some goods. Equipped items and unaffected inventory were checked independently.

The compact indicator is designed to appear only in menus and return to the hotkey hint 1.5 seconds after completion. Hiding during gameplay and F8 operation are verified; positive menu/Inventory visibility and the on-screen timeout still need final validation. F8 works independently of the indicator.

Use offline only. Official online/Easy Anti-Cheat, multiplayer, storage selling and future game versions are unsupported. Native calls run on a worker thread: do not travel, collect items or change equipment while selling. Uninstalling does not restore sold items. Keep a save backup.
