[b]Elden Ring Duplicate Seller - offline beta[/b]

[b]AI-generated.[/b] Code and docs written by AI coding agents. No independent review. Offline only. Back up your save.

Press F8 to sell surplus carried equipment at trader rune value, using the bundled price table or one extracted from your active regulation.

[b]Kept[/b]
• 2 per weapon/shield family.
• 2 per Ash of War.
• 1 per armor piece and talisman ID.
• All consumables, spells, crafting materials, key items, ammunition.

Equipped copies are always kept, even above the limit. Higher weapon upgrades are kept next. Affinities share one family. Talisman tiers and altered armor IDs count separately. Items traders refuse stay. Storage is not touched.

[b]Requirements[/b]
Windows x64, Elden Ring executable 2.7.1.0 (in-game 1.17.1), Mod Engine 2, Microsoft Visual C++ 2015-2022 x64 runtime. Tested with Randomizer 0.12.2 and Seamless Co-op 2.0.1 loaded, no other players connected. Those mods are not included.

[b]Install[/b]
Close the game. Back up your save.
1. Extract the ZIP. Run Install.cmd in ERDuplicateSeller.
2. Select your Mod Engine 2 config_eldenring.toml. Choose selling or preview.
3. Launch offline modded, load a character, stand still, press F8.

The installer registers the DLL and finds the price file. No manual editing. Other mods kept; changed files backed up. Settings.cmd switches preview/selling. Update: run Install.cmd from the new download (settings and prices kept). Uninstall.cmd removes registration and moves files to a backup.

[b]Prices and randomizers[/b]
Bundled table: from the base-game regulation. Equipment/Ash prices match the tested randomizer profile. If your overhaul or randomizer changes prices or IDs, extract a new table from the regulation you load with the source repository helper. regulation.bin is never replaced.

[b]Beta status[/b]
111 verified catalog test batches: 2,538 surplus equipment/Ash copies sold for exactly 1,221,770 runes. Item caps prevented 3-copy tests of some goods. Equipment and unaffected inventory checked independently.

The indicator should appear only in menus and revert to the hotkey hint 1.5 s after a run. Hidden in gameplay and F8 verified; menu/Inventory visibility and on-screen timeout not yet verified. F8 works regardless.

Offline only. Unsupported: official online/Easy Anti-Cheat, multiplayer, storage selling, future game versions. Selling runs on a worker thread: do not travel, pick up items or change equipment during a run. Uninstalling does not restore sold items. Keep a save backup.
